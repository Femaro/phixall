# Phixall.us - US Corporate Facility Management Design Specification

## Overview
Phixall.us is the corporate-focused US facility management platform powered by Phixall Facility Management LLC. This site targets commercial and residential facility management with a professional, corporate aesthetic while maintaining brand consistency with phixall.com.

## Domain Configuration
- **Primary Domain**: phixall.us
- **Target Market**: United States (Commercial & Residential)
- **Brand**: Phixall Facility Management LLC

## Design Philosophy

### Corporate Aesthetics
1. **Professional & Trustworthy**
   - Clean, structured layouts
   - Business-oriented color scheme (navy, charcoal, professional blues)
   - Emphasis on data, metrics, and ROI
   - Professional photography and industrial imagery

2. **Brand Consistency**
   - Maintains Phixall core brand elements (logo, typography)
   - Adapts color palette for corporate appeal
   - Shares design patterns but with more structured layouts
   - More formal tone and messaging

3. **Visual Hierarchy**
   - Clear information architecture
   - Data-driven presentations
   - Testimonials from corporate clients
   - Case studies and white papers

## Design Components

### Color Palette (Corporate)
- **Primary Navy**: #1e3a5f (trust, professionalism)
- **Secondary Charcoal**: #2c3e50
- **Accent Blue**: #3498db (less vibrant than phixall.com)
- **Success Green**: #27ae60
- **Warning Orange**: #e67e22
- **Neutrals**: Gray scale from #f8f9fa to #212529

### Typography
- **Headings**: Inter, Poppins (bold, professional)
- **Body**: System fonts, Roboto (readable, corporate)
- **Numbers/Data**: Monospace fonts for metrics

### Layout Patterns
- **Grid-Based**: Structured, aligned layouts
- **Card Designs**: Clean cards with subtle shadows
- **Data Dashboards**: Charts, graphs, KPIs
- **Full-Width Sections**: Alternating backgrounds
- **Sidebar Navigation**: For service pages
- **Mega Menus**: For complex service hierarchies

### Components
1. **Service Cards**: Icon, title, brief description, CTA
2. **Stats Counters**: Animated numbers with labels
3. **Industry Badges**: Certification and compliance logos
4. **Testimonial Cards**: Corporate client quotes with company logos
5. **Case Study Cards**: Image, title, industry, results summary
6. **Quote Request Forms**: Multi-step with service selection
7. **Coverage Maps**: Interactive US map
8. **Pricing Tables**: Enterprise tiers with feature comparison
9. **Resource Cards**: White papers, guides with download CTAs
10. **Team Cards**: Leadership profiles
